Product pages should include schema.org Product structured data as JSON-LD, with the @type set to "Product". This allows search engines to show rich results such as price and star ratings directly in search listings.

The Product schema should include an "offers" field containing price, priceCurrency, and availability. Missing offers data means the product cannot show a price snippet in search results.

Including "aggregateRating" or "review" fields in the Product schema enables star-rating rich snippets in search results, which significantly improve click-through rate from search.

Product titles should be descriptive and accurately match the product being sold. A title that does not match the product description or images can hurt both SEO relevance and customer trust, and may trigger marketplace policy violations.

Duplicate or near-duplicate product descriptions across many product pages can be treated as low-quality or duplicate content by search engines, hurting rankings for all affected pages.
