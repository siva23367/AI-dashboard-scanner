A word flagged by a dictionary spell-checker is not automatically a real spelling mistake.

Before marking any word as an error, determine whether it is:

* A brand name (Nike, Samsung, Apple, Adidas)
* A company name
* A product name or model number (Galaxy Z Fold, Air Max, ThinkPad X1)
* A person's name
* A place name
* A technical term (Bluetooth, HDMI, Kubernetes, PostgreSQL)
* A programming language, framework, library, API, or software product
* An acronym or abbreviation
* A unit of measurement (kg, cm, ml, GHz, TB)
* A domain-specific term used in business, healthcare, finance, law, education, science, engineering, cybersecurity, gaming, or social media
* A coined marketing term, feature name, or trademarked phrase

Do not flag such words merely because they are missing from a standard dictionary.

A word should be marked as a spelling error only when there is strong evidence that it is an unintentional misspelling of a common word and the intended correction is reasonably clear from context.

Examples of genuine spelling mistakes:

* recieve → receive
* comftable → comfortable
* enviroment → environment
* seperately → separately
* sucessful → successful

When uncertain, prefer treating the word as valid rather than incorrectly flagging a legitimate brand, technical term, product name, or proper noun.

Use surrounding context to determine whether the word is intentional. If the sentence remains meaningful as a product name, feature name, technical term, company name, username, hashtag, or domain-specific term, do not report it as a spelling issue.

Additionally check for grammar issues including:

* Subject–verb agreement errors
* Missing articles (a, an, the)
* Incorrect verb tense usage
* Run-on sentences
* Sentence fragments
* Repeated words
* Missing punctuation that affects readability
* Incorrect capitalization of ordinary words

Report only high-confidence spelling and grammar mistakes. Minimize false positives and prioritize accuracy over the number of issues reported.
